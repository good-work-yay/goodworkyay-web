Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Lull is a puzzle app by GoodWork, LLC, trading as Good Work Yay ("we," "our," or "us"). This policy explains what Lull does with your data. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. Lull has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not connect to our servers.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Lull saves your settings (screen dimness, animation preference) and which levels you have solved. This is kept only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is removed when you delete the app or tap "Start over" in Settings.

3. Third-Party Services

Lull contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. Apple may collect crash and usage information under its own privacy policy if you have opted in to share analytics with app developers.

4. Children's Privacy

Lull collects no information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes to This Policy

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

6. Contact Us

Questions? Reach us at hello@goodworkyay.com.
