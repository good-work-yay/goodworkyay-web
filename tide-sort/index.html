Tide Sort

Calm water color puzzles for winding down

Pour softly coloured water between glass tubes until each tube holds a single colour. No timers, no move counter and no scores — just 300 quiet puzzles in a low-glare night palette, with optional sleep music made for winding down before bed.

How to play

  • Tap a tube to lift it, then tap another tube to pour.
  • You can pour onto an empty tube or onto the same colour, as much as fits.
  • Fill every tube with a single colour to finish the level.
  • Stuck? Undo, Restart, or tap Hint to see one good pour.
  • Colour marks (Settings) add a small shape to each colour, so you never have to rely on colour alone.
