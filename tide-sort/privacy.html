Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Tide Sort is a puzzle app by GoodWork, LLC, trading as Good Work Yay ("we," "our," or "us"). It is free to play and supported by ads. This policy explains what data is involved and who handles it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. Tide Sort has no accounts or sign-in, and the app does not send your game data to our servers.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your settings (screen dimness, animation, colour marks and sleep music preferences) and which levels you have solved are kept only in the app's local storage on your device. They are removed when you delete the app or tap "Start over" in Settings.

3. Advertising

Tide Sort shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers — the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the app asks.
  • Usage and diagnostic data — ad interactions, approximate location derived from IP address, device type and operating system.

If you do not allow tracking, Google serves ads that are not personalized using your advertising identifier. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice anytime under Settings → Ad privacy. Google's use of this data is described in its How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services page.

4. Children's Privacy

Tide Sort is not directed at children under 13, and we limit ads to content rated suitable for general audiences.

5. Changes to This Policy

If this changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Questions? Reach us at hello@goodworkyay.com.
