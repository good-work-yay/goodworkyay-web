Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

ScriptDrill is an app by GoodWork, LLC, trading as Good Work Yay ("we," "our," or "us"). This policy explains what data ScriptDrill collects, why we collect it, and how we handle it. By using ScriptDrill, you agree to these practices.

1. Information We Collect

ScriptDrill collects the following types of information:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you create an account.
  • Voice and audio recordings — recordings you make in the app while practicing scripts. These are used solely to generate pronunciation and delivery feedback for you.
  • Usage data — anonymized information about how you use the app (e.g., features used, session length, error logs) to help us improve ScriptDrill.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver pronunciation and delivery feedback by processing your audio recordings.
  • To manage your account and send you important service-related communications.
  • To analyze aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to improve the app's features and performance.

3. Audio Recordings

Audio you record in ScriptDrill is transmitted to our servers and processed by third-party AI services to generate feedback. We take the following steps to protect your audio:

  • Audio is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS).
  • We do not sell your audio or use it to train AI models without your explicit consent.
  • Recordings are deleted from our servers after processing unless you explicitly save them within the app.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party providers for cloud infrastructure and AI-powered audio analysis. These providers access your data only to perform services on our behalf and are contractually required to keep it confidential and secure. We do not share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing hello@goodworkyay.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

ScriptDrill is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently done so, please contact us and we will delete the information promptly.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission and access controls. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@goodworkyay.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page or by sending an email to your registered address.

10. Contact Us

Questions or concerns about your privacy? Reach us at hello@goodworkyay.com.
