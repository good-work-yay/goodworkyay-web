About us

Good Work Yay is a small independent software studio. We build mobile apps that help people practice something difficult until it feels easy.

Who we are

Good Work Yay is the trade name of GoodWork, LLC, a limited liability company founded in 2026. This website, goodworkyay.com, is the official website of GoodWork, LLC, and the apps listed on it are published by GoodWork, LLC.

We are a small team. There is no sales department and no support queue three layers deep — when you email us, you reach someone who works on the app.

What we build

Our apps sit in one category: deliberate practice. The kind of skill you cannot read your way into, only rehearse your way into.

  • ScriptDrill — an AI pronunciation coach. Write or paste any script, record yourself, and get immediate feedback on pronunciation, rhythm, and delivery. Built for interviews, presentations, and conversations you want to get right the first time.

More apps are in development. We would rather release one app we are proud of than five we are not.

How we work

Focused. Every app does one thing properly. We cut features that dilute the core of the product, even good ones.

Private by default. We collect the minimum data needed to make an app work. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Our privacy policy spells out exactly what that means.

Reachable. Support email is answered by people who build the software, usually within two business days.
