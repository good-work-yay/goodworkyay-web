Dot Threads

Calm line puzzles for winding down

Connect matching dots with soft threads and fill every square. No timers and no scores — just 400 quiet puzzles in a low-glare night palette made for winding down before bed.

How to play

  • Drag from a dot to its matching dot to lay a thread.
  • Threads can't cross. Every square on the board must be filled.
  • Stuck? Undo, Restart, or tap Hint to place one thread for you.
